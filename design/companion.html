Companion — redesign mockups

Round 3: the behaviour is settled (layout A, no pause, pill jumps without filtering, pinned panels shrink to 3 lines, right-click menu, whole-line selection), so this round only changes the look. Pick a style below; every style is the same working panel. Nothing is wired to the real app.

1 · The mascot, with no box behind it

Just the pixel spawner on your desktop. Its flame is the status light: it spawns pixels when apps print, turns red when one fails, and goes grey and tucks into the screen edge when nothing happens. The only box that ever appears is the error pill, because it carries text.

2 · The panel, in five styles

It starts pinned with the pointer away, so you see the compact state: the last 3 lines under the current filter. Hover it to expand to where you were. Unpin it (the pin icon) and it hides when you move away instead. Right-click an app for restart and stop. Selecting works as in Spawner: click a line to pick it, drag across lines to pick all of them, or drag inside one line for normal text selection. To try the pill: crash payments-worker, unpin, move off the panel so it hides, then click the pill.

3 · “Open Spawner” returns the exact view

The companion keeps the same view state the main window uses, so the two are one place seen from two sizes. Scroll up in the companion, focus an app, set a filter, then open Spawner: you land on the same app, filter, and line, not at the bottom of everything.

    Decided

    • Layout A. No pause: apps are restarted or stopped from a right-click menu on their chip (or on “All apps” for all of them).
    • The error pill (“error about 1 min ago”, counting) opens the panel and scrolls to the failing line without touching your filters, so the other apps' lines around it stay visible. Only when a filter would hide that line are the filters cleared, with a toast saying so.
    • Selection is ported from the main window: click toggles a line, dragging across lines selects whole lines (and scrolls at the edges), dragging inside one line keeps the browser's own highlight, and a “N lines · Copy” bar appears (Esc clears).
    • “Loses focus” means the pointer leaves the panel. The companion always hides while the main Spawner window is focused, pinned or not.
    • Pinned and pointer away: compact, 3 lines, same filter, with the filter named in the header. Hover brings back the full panel at the scroll position you left it. Collapse and expand are equally quick (0.15 s wait, 0.16 s animation).
